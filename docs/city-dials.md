# City dials: Paris, Madrid, Milan and Santiago

Batch A, October 18, 2026. Forty-two stations already in Dial were real FM broadcasters but sat under each country's Other stations, because no local frequency had been checked. Each now has its city's FM frequency, so Paris, Madrid, Milan and Santiago get a tuning dial. No station was added or removed, no stream URL changed, and every station ID is unchanged, so favorites and saved stations still work.

## Rules

- A frequency is used only when two independent sources agree on it for that city.
- For networks with several transmitters, Dial uses the one that serves the city itself: the Tour Eiffel in Paris, Torrespaña in Madrid, and the transmitters inside Milan rather than the regional mountain sites (Valcava, Monte Penice). The profile notes say so.
- The streams are unchanged. For the national networks (all but NRJ Paris and the Santiago stations), the stream is the network feed that the city frequency carries, so local programme windows on air may not reach the stream. Each profile notes this.
- Catalunya Ràdio and RAC1 broadcast in Catalonia, not Madrid, so they stay under Spain's Other stations.

## Choices and limits

- **Milan over Rome:** most of these networks are based in Milan, and every station has a transmitter inside the city. Rome's lists were less clear-cut for Radio Italia, Radio Deejay and Virgin Radio.
- **Milan's other frequencies:** Italian networks use several frequencies around Milan (RTL 102.5 also uses 91.3, 92.2 and 102.5, for example). Dial shows one.
- **Radio Carolina's stream** is a Zeno.fm feed listed in Radio Browser; it hasn't been confirmed as the broadcaster's own.
- **Radio Beethoven** is on 97.7, not the 96.5 sometimes quoted elsewhere; both Santiago sources agree on 97.7.
- **Streams weren't rechecked:** the stream URLs are unchanged from their earlier checks.
